    Questions

    Do I need a prescription?{{icon:chev}}

    Yes. Every medicine here is a prescription medicine. Upload a photo or PDF of a valid prescription, and a pharmacist checks it before your order is packed.

    Can I reuse a prescription I uploaded before?{{icon:chev}}

    Yes, as long as it is still valid. Pick it at checkout instead of uploading it again.

    How can I pay?{{icon:chev}}

    Online through Razorpay, or cash on delivery for orders up to ₹5,000.

    What happens if my prescription is rejected?{{icon:chev}}

    You pay when you place the order. If the pharmacist can’t approve the prescription, we tell you why by SMS or email and refund your payment.

    Can I cancel or return an order?{{icon:chev}}

    You can cancel any time before the order is packed. Medicines can be returned only if they arrive damaged, wrong or expired.

    Why do some packs say Win2Age Pharma?{{icon:chev}}

    Win2Age Pharma is Aarvion’s earlier marketing name. Some product photos still show packs printed under it until photos of the Aarvion packs are ready.
