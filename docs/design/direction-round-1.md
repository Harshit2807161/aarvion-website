# Home page: direction round 1 (Impeccable new-work, Persuade mode)

Seed reference: `seed:26efee81f47ef0ec`. Assigned index 4. The owner delegated the choice ("go with the best design… I'll look tomorrow"), so this round is decided by me and recorded here instead of on Impeccable's decision page.

**Pinned by the brief (binding):**
- forest green + white palette; sand beige #E8DCC4 as a quiet secondary is allowed (Fernwood ref);
- modern, sleek, soothing;
- references: Fernwood, Leon Abramovic, the wide-type bento layout.

## Step 1: grounding
- **Mechanism:** the marketer sells its own medicines directly. Genuine packs at a fixed ₹20 below printed MRP, free delivery, pharmacist-checked prescriptions.
- **Scene:** an adult child in an Indian city, evening, on a mid-range Android phone, reordering a parent's heart or eye medicine, the paper prescription on the table beside them.
- **Cultural home:** the Indian chemist counter and the medicine pack itself (strip, carton, leaflet, the dot-matrix MRP/batch stamp).
- **What the first surface must prove:** these are real Aarvion packs; the price math (MRP − ₹20) is exact and honest; a pharmacist checks the Rx; ordering is easy.
- **The rut:**
  - the category page: a blue/white e-pharmacy with discount banners, coupon chips, an icon-tile category grid and a row of equal cards;
  - its predictable opposite: a cream-and-serif wellness DTC (botanical, Fernwood-literal).

## Step 2: seven grounded systems (by resonance)
1. **Blister strip + dot-matrix MRP stamp:** silver foil, white carton, inkjet dot-matrix batch/MRP block, perforations. The place every Indian already checks the price.
2. **Doctor's Rx pad:** ℞ letterhead, registration number, ruled lines, the tear-off sheet.
3. **Neighbourhood chemist counter:** green cross sign, glass counter of strips in compartments, handwritten tags.
4. **Folded package-insert leaflet:** a numbered-section monograph (Composition, Dosage, Storage), tables, fold creases.
5. **Misty botanical garden** (Lodhi/Sunder Nursery at dawn): fog and layered greens, the Fernwood mood.
6. **Weekly pill organiser:** morning/evening compartments, the refill ritual.
7. **Courier AWB slip:** barcode, stamped statuses, the tracking journey.

Material families: printed packaging (1, 4), paper forms (2, 7), places (3, 5), objects (6).

## Cards

### ASSIGNED: "The Leaflet" (grounded #4)
- **Thesis:** The site is set like the clearest medicine leaflet ever printed. Numbered sections, composition tables, exact facts, and the price line is part of the fact table. It refuses the discount-banner grid.
- **World:**
  - a forest-green carton field (#1F3D2B family) wrapping white leaflet panels;
  - fold creases as section dividers;
  - sand (#E8DCC4) as the paper's reverse, used sparingly;
  - a modern tight grotesk display, a clean sans body, a mono for batch-like data.
- **First viewport:** a deep forest field. A large white leaflet panel slides out from behind the real Sacuwin 100 carton. On it:
  - the headline "Genuine medicines, ₹20 below MRP";
  - a working search (brand or salt) with strength chips;
  - a live fact row: Sacuwin 100 · 14 tablets · MRP ₹407 · you pay ₹387;
  - the primary action "Upload prescription".
- **Signature interaction:** product details unfold like leaflet panels along their creases.
- **Raises:** all six above.
- **Risk:** numbered sections + hairlines can drift into the editorial-hairline cluster. It must stay roomy and sleek.

### IMPECCABLE'S PICK: "The Strip" (grounded #1)
- **Thesis:** The price promise is printed where Indians already check it: the dot-matrix MRP block on the pack, then struck and re-stamped ₹20 lower.
- **World:**
  - forest green + white;
  - products sit in a horizontal "strip" of blister pockets with perforations between them;
  - a fine dot field (Fernwood's texture, here read as inkjet print);
  - a mono for the MRP/batch stamp.
- **First viewport:** a big centred headline on a forest field with dot grain. Below it a strip of the real packs in pockets, each stamped "MRP ₹407 · ₹387". A search pill and Upload Rx.
- **Risk:** the familiar Fernwood-like centred hero; the dot texture can read as decoration.
